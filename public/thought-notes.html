Platform support

Same local notes. Different capture options.

iOS

Text and pasted notes

Capture typed thoughts or paste text, then organize notes with an optional on-device model. Voice capture is not included in the iOS v1 release.

Android

Text, paste, and user-started voice

Record from the app or home-screen widget when you choose. Whisper transcribes speech on device. Recording does not run in the background.

Data handling

Your notes stay in local app storage.

Thought Notes has no publisher account, ads, analytics, cloud AI backend, or sync service. Note organization and Android speech transcription run on device. An internet connection is used to download the optional AI model. The app does not intentionally include note text or topic files in model-download requests; hosts still receive ordinary connection details such as your IP address and the requested model URL. Sharing happens only when you choose an app in the system share sheet.

Good to know

Before you start

Does Thought Notes send notes to a cloud AI service?

No. Note synthesis runs on device. The app does use the network to download the optional model, and the model host receives normal request metadata.

Can I capture notes without the AI model?

Yes. Typed notes and pasted text are saved to Inbox first. If the model is missing, the device is under resource pressure, or processing fails, the original remains available there.

How do I enable on-device topic organization?

Install or manage the recommended model in Settings. It is about 585 MB and targets devices with at least 4 GB of RAM. Eligible devices may start downloading it automatically; note capture still works while it is unavailable.

Can I record voice notes on iPhone?

No. The current iOS release supports typed notes and pasted text. User-started voice capture with local transcription is available on Android.

Is Thought Notes available in the app stores?

Not yet. Public App Store and Google Play listings are not live. Check the project repository for source and setup information.
